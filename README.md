# 🛡️ CallGuardAI - AI-Powered Call Protection

An AI-powered call protection prototype designed to explore how artificial intelligence can help identify suspicious calls and potential scam attempts. CallGuardAI focuses on improving awareness of fraudulent communication and helping users make safer decisions.

## 📌 Project Overview

Phone scams and social engineering attacks can trick people into sharing sensitive information, revealing credentials, or making fraudulent payments.

**CallGuardAI** explores the use of AI-driven analysis to identify potentially suspicious call patterns and support scam-risk assessment. The project aims to make call interactions safer by helping users recognize possible warning signs.

This prototype demonstrates the potential of combining artificial intelligence with communication security to address real-world digital safety challenges.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Explore AI-based approaches to suspicious call identification.
- Identify potential indicators of scam-related communication.
- Improve awareness of social engineering and fraudulent calls.
- Design a prototype for AI-assisted call protection.
